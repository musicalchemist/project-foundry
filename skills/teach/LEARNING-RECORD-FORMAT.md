# Learning record

Create `learning-records/0001-slug.md` in the agreed workspace when an insight will change future teaching; increment the highest existing number.

```md
# Concept learned or misconception corrected

Evidence: What the learner demonstrated and what help they needed.
Implication: What they can attempt next or should revisit.
```

Record demonstrated understanding, a corrected misconception, disclosed prior knowledge with its claimed depth, or a changed mission. Do not log every topic covered or infer mastery from an agent-written answer.

When later work revises the understanding, link a superseding record. Keep glossary definitions in the glossary rather than duplicating them in learning records.
