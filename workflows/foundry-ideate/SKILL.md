# Compare testable ideas

For an existing idea, sharpen its problem and first result before proposing alternatives. Otherwise generate options against the user's stated domain, resources, and decision criteria.

For each option specify: audience/question, simplest useful result, non-AI/non-custom baseline, required data and rights, largest uncertainty, effort/cost drivers, and a cheap falsification test. Use a premortem to expose the failure that would make building it wasteful.

Compare by the user's priorities; avoid numeric scores without a defined scale. Separate evidence from assumptions and verify uncertain external capabilities.

Return a recommended option, why it wins under the constraints, what would reverse the recommendation, and one experiment with a time/compute cap, pass/fail evidence, and follow-up decision. Narrowing or parking is a valid result.
