# Produce reproducible evidence

Before analysis, record the question, exploratory/confirmatory status, sampling unit, comparator, outcome/estimand, data rights, and permitted compute/provider destinations.

For literature work, use primary evidence and maintain a claim-to-source table with design, support, and limitations. Bound the search; record unanswered questions.

For analysis, inspect grain, missingness, duplicates, units, and dependence. Specify confounding, selection, multiplicity, leakage, and batch-effect handling where applicable. Preserve raw inputs and lineage; separate exploratory tuning from confirmation.

Record code/environment/data versions, parameters/seeds, exact entry command, compute cap, and artifact paths. Test numerical logic against known cases or independent calculations with justified tolerances. Biological studies also need assay/reference/annotation and replicate metadata.

Return protocol, execution evidence, result uncertainty, alternative explanations, limitations, and the next decision. A negative or inconclusive result can complete the question; execution alone cannot validate a claim.
