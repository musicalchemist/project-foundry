# Sources and adaptations

The 16 workflows in `skills/` are adapted from [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). The seven workflows `grill-me`, `grilling`, `codebase-design`, `tdd`, `diagnosing-bugs`, `writing-for-agents`, and `pr` derive from [revision 24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888). The other nine are unpinned adaptations; no exact upstream revision or byte identity is asserted for them.

This edition uses local documents and selected tasks instead of mandatory tracker publication, automatic commits, delegated reviewers, or helper execution. TDD's review step points to the included `code-review` skill. Supporting Markdown is retained; executable helpers and host interface metadata are omitted. The files are adaptations, not an untouched repository snapshot.

[Matt Pocock's MIT license](skills/LICENSE) applies to the Matt-derived skills and their supporting text. Retain the full notice with redistributed copies of that material. [PR credits](skills/pr/CREDITS.md) preserve the additional Dex Horthy/HumanLayer attribution. The original project guidance in `about.md` and `best-practices.md` is separate from those skills.
